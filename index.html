Built For Real-World Platform Resilience

How our ingestion design accounts for detection surface vectoring without fragile hacks.

Gaussian Pacing & Jitter

Bots fail when their request intervals are predictable. We apply natural Gaussian probability distributions with jitter, avoiding rigid sleep loops that scream automation.

TLS & JA3 Alignment

Modern anti-bot gateways inspect the TLS handshake before reading HTTP headers. Our pipeline matches client-hello cipher suites to the exact User-Agent declared.

Circuit Breakers & Fallbacks

When a target site alters markup overnight or rate-limits requests, our system automatically demotes to RSS feeds, sitemaps, or cached fallbacks instead of crashing silently.

Ethical Scope & Boundaries

Respecting site infrastructure is mandatory. We enforce strict request rate caps, respect robots.txt advisories, and avoid unauthorized account creation or credential abuse.
